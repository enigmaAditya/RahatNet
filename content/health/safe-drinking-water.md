## Why Water Purification Is Essential

During natural catastrophes such as floods, cyclones, and earthquakes, municipal water pipes frequently fracture and surface wells become inundated with sewage, soil, and dead organic matter. Drinking contaminated water can rapidly trigger outbreaks of cholera, typhoid, and dysentery.

---

## 1. Rolling Boil Method (Most Reliable)

Boiling is the single most effective method to eliminate bacteria, viruses, and parasites.

- Strain cloudy or turbid water through a clean cotton cloth or folded sari into a clean pot.
- Bring the water to a vigorous, rolling boil for **at least 1 full minute** (3 minutes if at high altitude).
- Allow the water to cool naturally in the same vessel. Keep the container covered with a clean lid or cloth to protect against airborne contaminants and insect vectors.

---

## 2. Chlorine Purification Tablets

When fuel or wood for boiling is unavailable, chlorine tablets (Halazone or Sodium Dichloroisocyanurate) are widely distributed by relief missions.

- Ensure water is strained clear of heavy mud before adding chemical tablets.
- Add the tablet according to the specific manufacturer or relief packet dosage (typically one tablet per 4 to 5 litres of water).
- Stir thoroughly and let the treated water stand covered for **at least 30 minutes** before drinking. This contact time is critical to neutralize pathogens.

---

## Safe Storage Practices

- Store purified water only in cleaned, narrow-necked jugs or vessels with fitted lids.
- Do not dip hands, cups, or unsterilized utensils directly into stored drinking vessels. Pour water out from the top or use a vessel equipped with an uncontaminated clean spigot.
- Separate drinking water containers distinctly from water intended for bathing and washing livestock.

---

*Note: Always follow instructions and advisories issued by your local disaster management authorities and emergency response teams.*
