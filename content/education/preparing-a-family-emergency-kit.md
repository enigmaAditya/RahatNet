## Why Every Household Needs a 72-Hour Kit

During major cyclones, flash floods, earthquakes, or industrial chemical leaks, first responders may take up to 72 hours to reach every isolated pocket. Having a packed, waterproof "Go-Bag" ensures your family has the basic provisions needed to survive independently during the critical initial days.

---

## Essential Supplies Checklist

Keep supplies packed in a sturdy, water-resistant backpack placed in an easily accessible location near your main exit.

### 1. Water and Nutrition
- **Drinking Water:** Pack clean water in tightly sealed, unbreakable bottles (aim for at least 2 to 3 litres per person).
- **Non-Perishable Food:** High-energy foods that require no cooking or refrigeration: roasted grams (chana), flattened rice (poha), dates, jaggery, biscuits, and energy bars.
- Compact manual can opener and lightweight utensils.

### 2. Medical and Hygiene Essentials
- First-aid box containing sterile gauze pads, antiseptic lotion, adhesive bandages, scissors, and a clinical thermometer.
- **Critical Prescriptions:** At least 7 days' supply of ongoing family medications (hypertension, asthma inhalers, insulin, heart medications) in a labeled zip-lock pouch.
- Soap bars, sanitary napkins, chlorine water purification tablets, and hand sanitizer.

### 3. Light and Communications
- Battery-powered LED flashlight or solar hand-crank torch.
- Portable AM/FM radio with extra sealed batteries to listen to official broadcasts.
- Fully charged power bank with appropriate mobile charging cables.
- High-decibel whistle to signal search and rescue teams if trapped under debris.

### 4. Important Documents in Waterproof Pouches
Keep laminated photocopies or sealed original copies of:
- Government identity cards (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Ration Card).
- Property deeds, land ownership papers, and bank passbooks.
- Recent family photographs to assist authorities if family members are separated.
- Emergency cash in small denominations (ATMs and electronic UPI payments will not function without power and internet).

---

## Maintenance: The Semi-Annual Kit Check

Every six months (for instance, right before the annual monsoon season begins):
- Check expiration dates on packaged food rations and medicines.
- Test batteries in torches and radios.
- Rotate and replace stored bottled drinking water with fresh supplies.

---

*Note: Always follow instructions and advisories issued by your local disaster management authorities and emergency response teams.*
